# Code of Conduct

We want Matryca Trama to be calm, constructive, and welcoming.

Participants must be respectful, assume good faith, give useful technical feedback, and protect private information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and sharing someone else's private material are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's published contact channel.
