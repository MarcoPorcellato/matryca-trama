# Commercial Licensing

The PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 does not grant use for commercial
purposes. Commercial business, enterprise, client-service, hosted-service, OEM,
or other commercial use requires a separate written agreement from the
licensor. The licence separately permits the noncommercial purposes and named
noncommercial organisations stated in its exact text, regardless of their
funding source.

Commercial licences are not yet generally available and no price is promised
by this repository. Future offers may use published self-service prices,
private quotations, subscriptions, perpetual terms, bundles, or negotiated
enterprise agreements.

An enquiry or quotation is not permission to use the software commercially.
Commercial rights begin only under the terms of an executed agreement. Until a
dedicated sales channel exists, open a non-confidential licensing discussion
through the repository support channel. Do not include secrets, customer data,
or private procurement information in a public issue.

See [the licensing model](docs/LICENSING_MODEL.md) for the current product
boundary. This page is an operational summary; the licence and any executed
commercial agreement control.

This summary is not legal advice. Borderline purposes should be reviewed against
the exact licence and, when necessary, by qualified counsel.
