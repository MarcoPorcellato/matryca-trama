# Contributor Licensing

Matryca Trama intends to grant Community rights under PolyForm Noncommercial
1.0.0 and to offer separate commercial rights. The project therefore needs
clear permission to distribute accepted contributions through both paths.

No lawyer-reviewed contributor licence agreement is active yet. Until one is
adopted, maintainers will not merge external code or documentation
contributions into the product tree. Contributors remain welcome to:

- report bugs and accessibility problems;
- propose requirements and designs;
- discuss architecture and use cases;
- review public changes; and
- contribute non-copyrightable factual observations.

Opening an issue or participating in discussion does not transfer copyright.
Do not attach third-party source, private vault content, credentials, customer
data, or material whose provenance is uncertain.

The future contributor agreement must be reviewed for the applicable
jurisdictions, remain understandable to contributors, preserve contributor
copyright, and grant the permissions needed for both noncommercial Community
distribution and commercial licensing. A DCO alone will not be treated as that
relicensing grant unless legal review explicitly establishes it.
