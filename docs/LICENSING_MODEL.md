# Matryca Trama Licensing Model

## Current decision

Matryca Trama uses a dual-path model:

1. repository-owned Community material is publicly readable and licensed under
   PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0; and
2. commercial use requires a separate written licence from the licensor.

This is a source-available model, not an OSI-approved open-source model. The
licence text in `LICENSE` controls. This document explains product intent and
does not replace legal terms.

## What remains available without payment

PolyForm Noncommercial permits the purposes stated in its exact text, including
the described personal uses and uses by the named noncommercial organisations.
It also permits changes and distribution only for permitted purposes and
requires preservation of the licence and `Required Notice:` lines.

Do not infer that every unpaid activity is noncommercial, or that every paid
role is automatically excluded. The licence expressly permits its named
noncommercial organisations regardless of funding. Work for a commercial
purpose, including commercial client, consultancy, hosted-service, or
anticipated commercial application, needs a separate review and normally a
commercial licence.

## How commercial access may be sold later

The repository licence does not prescribe a price, billing system, public price
page, or licence-key technology. The project may later offer one or more of:

- a public fixed price for individuals or small teams;
- monthly or annual subscriptions;
- a perpetual licence with paid maintenance;
- per-user, per-device, per-workspace, or organisation-wide terms;
- a free trial followed by a paid licence;
- private quotes for enterprise, OEM, consultancy, hosting, or redistribution;
- a Trama Pro bundle, a Matryca Brain bundle, or separately priced products.

These choices may be made later through a pricing decision and commercial
agreement. Until then, there is no promised price, entitlement, renewal term,
service level, or generally available commercial licence.

Public prices are optional as a sales strategy. A self-service consumer checkout
must disclose the total price and required pre-contract information before the
purchase under applicable law. Negotiated business offers can instead use a
private quote. Legal and tax review is required before accepting payment.

## Dependency boundary

Matryca Parser, Matryca Plumber, Logseq, and other third-party components retain
their own licences. PolyForm Noncommercial applies only to repository-owned
material that does not state different terms. A dependency licence does not
automatically grant commercial rights to Trama, and Trama does not relicense
third-party code.

## Bootstrap history

The initial repository bootstrap commit contained only an introductory README
and an Apache-2.0 licence. Before product implementation or external
contributions, the foundation decision replaced that licence for future
repository-owned Trama material with PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0. Published
history is preserved rather than rewritten.

## Decisions still open

- commercial licence agreement and warranty terms;
- pricing metric, currency, taxes, refunds, renewals, and regional availability;
- public self-service checkout versus contact-sales offers;
- entitlement and offline licence verification;
- whether Trama Pro code is private, visible under separate terms, or delivered
  as a service;
- whether Brain includes Trama Pro rights or remains a separate purchase;
- a lawyer-reviewed contributor agreement supporting both licence paths.

Each decision requires its own review before it becomes a user-facing promise.
